## Introduction

Computer vision, a form of artificial intelligence, is transforming how
we extract species detections from large ecological datasets. This
workshop will focus on the task of species classification from image and
audio data, concentrating specifically on wildlife camera traps and
acoustic recorders, although the general principles are applicable to
other data modalities. In the workshop, we will cover general concepts
in computer vision, including how to evaluate and critically assess
model performance. We will then discuss practical tools for implementing
computer vision workflows, including how to choose among available
options, and demonstrate the use of two popular models: SpeciesNet (for
camera trap images) and BirdNET (for bioacoustics data). The workshop
will be structured in three parts: (1) general computer vision concepts,
(2) camera trap data processing, and (3) bioacoustics data processing.
